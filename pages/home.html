FAQ about organic food

Is organic food healthier than regular food?

Organic food may help reduce exposure to certain artificial additives and farming chemicals. However, overall health also depends on food variety, portion size, nutritional balance, and individual dietary needs.

How can I identify genuine organic products?

Check the product label, ingredients, source information, packaging, producer details, and available certifications. Buying from a trusted organic food shop with transparent product information can also reduce the risk of purchasing misleading products.

Where can I buy organic food in Bangladesh?

You can purchase organic food from trusted physical stores, farms, and online shops. Girosto offers a convenient selection of organic groceries, natural food items, and daily essentials for customers in Bangladesh.

Why is organic food more expensive?

Organic products may cost more because of smaller production volumes, natural farming methods, careful processing, specialized sourcing, and higher quality-control requirements. Seasonal availability and transportation can also affect the price.

Does organic food contain pesticides?

Organic farming generally limits the use of synthetic pesticides, but certain approved natural pest-control methods may still be used. Customers should review product details or ask the seller for sourcing information when necessary.

How should organic food be stored?

Storage depends on the product. Dry foods should be kept in airtight containers in a cool, dry place, while dairy products, fruits, and other perishable items may require refrigeration.

Is organic food suitable for children?

Many organic foods can be included in a child’s balanced diet when they are age-appropriate and prepared safely. Parents should consider allergies, nutritional needs, and professional medical advice when introducing new foods.
