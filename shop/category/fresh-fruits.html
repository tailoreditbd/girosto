Good to know

FAQ about fresh fruits

Bangladesh offers many seasonal fruits throughout the year, including mangoes, jackfruits, litchis, watermelons, guavas, pineapples, papayas, bananas and oranges. Availability depends on the harvesting period and regional supply.

Seasonal fruits are generally harvested during their natural growing period, which often gives them better taste, aroma and texture. They may also be more readily available and reasonably priced during peak season.

Storage depends on the type and ripeness of the fruit. Bananas, mangoes and papayas may be kept at room temperature until ripe, while grapes, oranges, apples and cut fruits should usually be refrigerated.
