FAQ about daily fresh food

Golden raisins are generally lighter in color with a mild, sweet taste, while black raisins usually have a deeper flavor and darker appearance. Kashmiri raisins may vary in color, size, and sweetness depending on the grape variety and processing method.

Soya chunks are usually soaked or boiled in hot water until soft, drained, and then added to curries, stir-fries, pulao, or other recipes. Following the preparation instructions on the packaging helps achieve a better texture.

Whole mushrooms and sweet corn can be used in soups, noodles, pasta, fried rice, pizza, salads, and mixed vegetable dishes. As canned products may contain liquid or brine, they should usually be drained before cooking.
