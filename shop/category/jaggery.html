Buy jaggery at Girosto

Jaggery is widely used in traditional sweets, pitha, payesh, desserts, and everyday recipes across Bangladesh. However, finding authentic jaggery with its natural aroma, flavor, and proper texture can be difficult because many products may contain excess sugar, artificial coloring, or inconsistent ingredients.

Girosto makes it easier to choose quality jaggery from a carefully selected collection. We offer sugarcane jaggery, date palm jaggery patali, nolen date palm jaggery, liquid date palm jaggery or jhola gur, and palmyra palm jaggery patali for different seasonal dishes and household preferences.
