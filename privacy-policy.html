Privacy policy

Your information, handled with care

A clear overview of the information used to process orders, provide support, and improve the Girosto experience.

This policy explains the personal information Girosto may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to customers and site visitors.

Information we may collect

We may collect your name, telephone number, email address, delivery address, order details, customer-service messages, and basic website usage information when you contact us or place an order.

How information is used

  • To confirm, prepare, deliver, and support orders.
  • To communicate about product availability, payment, or delivery.
  • To improve products, service, and website usability.
  • To prevent misuse and comply with applicable obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with delivery, payment, hosting, analytics, or support providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the service. Girosto does not sell customer personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential storage and measurement tools to understand visits and improve performance. Browser controls can be used to manage cookies, though some features may be affected.

Data retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for orders, support, records, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete personal information where applicable, and you may opt out of non-essential marketing communication.

Contact

For privacy questions, email girostobd@gmail.com or use the details on our contact page.
